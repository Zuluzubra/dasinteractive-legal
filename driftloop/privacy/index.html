Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Operator: DAS Interactive · Application ID: com.zuluzubra.driftloop

Questions about your information, or requests to access, correct or delete it, can be sent to dasinteractive.dev@gmail.com. For instructions that also work after uninstalling the app, see our Account & Data Deletion page.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

DAS Interactive operates DriftLoop, a mobile drifting and racing game. This policy describes the information handled by the current Android version, including Unity services used for guest identities, friends, multiplayer connectivity and diagnostic reporting. You do not need to provide an email address or password to register for the game's guest identity.

2. Guest identity and profile data

When you open DriftLoop, the game may create or resume a Unity Authentication guest identity to support social features. Unity assigns a persistent Player ID. We process the Player Name you choose or receive from Unity, authentication/session credentials, and service identifiers needed to keep your session working. Credentials are cached locally on your device. A guest account is not an identity-free account: its Player ID can be linked to player names, friends and activity. You should not put sensitive personal details in your Player Name.

3. Friends, online presence and multiplayer

When using social features, Unity Friends handles player names and IDs, friend requests and relationships, blocking, availability, in-game activity and structured game invitations. Other players may see your name, relevant status and shared game invitations. Exact-name friend lookups are sent to the service. The game does not access your phone contacts and does not currently offer free-text or voice chat.

Unity Multiplayer Services, Lobby, Relay, Netcode and Transport process online session details, matchmaking availability, connection identifiers and in-race updates. Other session participants can receive game-related information such as car movement, readiness, race timing, score, combo and virtual coin results. Public Quick Match sessions expose limited discovery information to other authenticated players; private Friendly Race sessions are restricted to participants.

4. Network information and approximate location

Unity's network infrastructure processes IP addresses and related connection information to deliver online services and select network regions. Unity's disclosures also identify approximate location processing for Friends, Lobby and Relay, including functionality and, for Relay, analytics purposes. DriftLoop does not request GPS location permission in the inspected Android release. Car positions and game-area labels refer to virtual gameplay, not your real-world location.

5. Diagnostics and device information

Unity Diagnostics records technical data to monitor service operation and investigate failures. Records observed for DriftLoop include a session identifier, installation identifier, session timestamps and duration, device model, Android operating system and API version, device RAM, screen resolution, game version and issue count. Depending on the event and configuration, crash reports, errors and performance measurements may also be processed. A Unity installation identifier is distinct from the Unity Authentication Player ID.

For DriftLoop, Unity Developer Data has been configured for Essential Data collection, without Additional Recommended Data. The enabled data uses are delivery/improvement of Unity products and services and Technical Support. Unity's underlying essential processing and the game's authentication and multiplayer services still process data. The current Android release configuration enables Unity Diagnostics. We do not use a separately integrated advertising SDK, in-app payment SDK or dedicated third-party tracking SDK in the audited version.

6. Local save data and Android backup

The game stores progression and preferences locally, including virtual coins, vehicle selections and unlocks, race records, mastery, achievements, daily challenges, vehicle maintenance, Idle Drift progress and audio/social preferences. Online race results are separately sent during multiplayer play, but DriftLoop does not use Cloud Save for the complete progression file in the audited version. Unity PlayerPrefs storage is not encrypted by the game.

The updated Android build configuration is designed to exclude the app's data from standard Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. As a result, uninstalling or moving to another phone may permanently lose local progress and guest sign-in access. Certain manufacturer-specific transfer methods or data previously backed up may behave differently; we cannot guarantee the removal of copies already held elsewhere.

7. Recipients, transfers and security

Data required for the described features is processed by Unity Technologies and its infrastructure/service providers. Relevant profile, social and race data is disclosed to other players when needed for game interactions. Email inquiries are processed through our support mailbox and its email service provider. Unity's own privacy information is available at Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Our inspected authentication and game-transport paths use mechanisms such as HTTPS, secure WebSockets and DTLS. Other infrastructure traffic, including region-quality probes, may use different mechanisms. We limit access to support requests and do not ask for passwords or access tokens by email. Services may process data internationally in accordance with applicable law and provider safeguards.

8. How long data is kept

Locally stored progress generally remains until app storage is removed or the deletion workflow clears the reviewed keys. Unity Authentication account information is ordinarily retained until the account is deleted. Unity's published default periods include up to 30 days for certain Friends gateway IP logs and Lobby/Relay records, and 90 days for Unity Diagnostics personal data. These periods apply to the specific service categories described by Unity; they are not a guarantee that every kind of data is deleted at the same time. Other service records, security logs, backups and support communications can have different retention requirements. We review such records and applicable exceptions when handling a request.

9. Your choices, access and deletion

You can edit your Player Name in Settings > Profile and manage your in-game friend relationships. To request account deletion, open Settings > Profile > Delete Account & Data and follow the confirmation steps. On a successful Unity Authentication deletion response, DriftLoop clears its reviewed local progression and preference keys. This operation does not itself establish that all associated Unity Friends, Lobby, Relay, diagnostic or backup records have been erased.

To request review or deletion of remaining associated data, or to make a request after uninstalling, email dasinteractive.dev@gmail.com and read the deletion request instructions. We must verify that you are authorized to act on the account before releasing or deleting identifiable remote records. A copied public Player ID or Player Name alone is insufficient evidence. Recovery or verification of an unlinked guest identity after credential loss may not always be possible. Some data may need to be retained where law or a legitimate safety/security requirement permits it; we will explain relevant limits and outcomes.

10. Children and international privacy rights

DriftLoop is not designed primarily for children. The intended age categories for the Google Play release will be identified in its store listing. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or information about the handling of your personal data. To exercise those rights, contact us using the email above. We may need to verify your request before taking account-specific action.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game's features, service settings or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be posted on this page. Privacy contact: dasinteractive.dev@gmail.com.
