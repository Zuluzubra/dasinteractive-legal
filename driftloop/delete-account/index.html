Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your DriftLoop guest account and associated information.

Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion even after uninstalling DriftLoop. Email our support contact using the button below.

Email deletion request

Or write to: dasinteractive.dev@gmail.com

Delete from the game

  1. Open DriftLoop → Settings → Profile → Delete Account & Data.
  2. Read the consequences and type DELETE to confirm.
  3. When Unity Authentication confirms deletion, the game removes the reviewed local progress and preferences. Follow the on-screen guidance to request deletion or review of additional service records.

What happens to your data?

The in-game process can delete your Unity Authentication guest account and reset this device's DriftLoop progress after successful confirmation. These include virtual coins, vehicle unlocks, race records, challenges, mastery and Idle Drift progress. Some Friends, multiplayer, network and diagnostic records are handled separately by Unity services and may require further review or deletion requests. Backups and service logs may have different retention periods.

If you no longer have the app

Send an email with the subject DriftLoop account and data deletion request. If you saved a non-secret deletion reference or Player ID from the app, you may include it. Describe whether you want your account deleted, associated data deleted, or both. If you cannot find the identifier, you may still contact us and explain the situation.

Verification and next steps

We must verify ownership before deleting or disclosing data tied to an identifiable account. A Player Name, copied ID or screenshot alone may not prove ownership. For unlinked guest accounts without surviving credentials, verification or recovery may not always be possible; we will explain any limitations and available alternatives. An email initiates a request; it does not automatically delete all remote information. We review the applicable Unity services and communicate the outcome, including any data that must be retained for legitimate reasons.

Do not email passwords, session tokens, access tokens, payment information or other sensitive credentials.

Support contact: dasinteractive.dev@gmail.com
